01By industry

Problem, product, availability.

Telecom operatorsConnectivity

Coverage and capacity are needed where the economics are least favourable: indoors, dense urban and low-ARPU areas. Each additional site carries power, backhaul and site-rental cost against uncertain traffic.

Small cells with lower power draw and bill of materials, changing the cost per site. Radio performance is delivered as firmware, so improvements do not require a site visit.

Gen 1 · in production
Manufacturing & private 5GConnectivity

Production lines are reconfigured regularly and cabling is the constraint on layout. Wireless has to provide bounded latency to carry control traffic, not just connectivity.

Private 5G networks on deterministic silicon, with the same platform from a single cell to a full site deployment.

Gen 1 · in production
Robotics & intralogisticsPerception

Mobile robots must run perception within a battery budget. Sending frames to central compute adds latency and consumes site bandwidth.

Perception processing in bounded time at battery-class power, alongside the application processor, with connectivity available from the same architecture.

Gen 2 · EVKs H2 2027
Quality & safety inspectionPerception

Inspection must run at line rate on every unit, at a cost per station that remains viable when multiplied across a plant.

Perception silicon at camera-class cost and power, with a pre-optimised model library that reduces time to first working inference.

Gen 2 · EVKs H2 2027
Energy & utilitiesBoth

Assets are remote, power is limited and frequently solar, and backhaul is expensive or unavailable. Processing has to happen locally.

Local processing and local connectivity from one architecture, scaling from sensor-class power to node-class capability.

Gen 1 → Gen 2
Smart infrastructureBoth

Camera and sensor deployments generate more data than available backhaul can carry, and data-protection rules increasingly require that raw streams remain on site.

Processing at the point of capture so that only results are transmitted, with connectivity from the same silicon family.

Gen 1 → Gen 2
Enterprise & campusConnectivity

Campus coverage is a capital decision taken against uncertain utilisation, and deployments stall when per-cell cost cannot be justified in advance.

Small cells at a cost point that allows coverage to be deployed ahead of demand, on a platform that scales with the site.
